# Third-Party Notices

## Hisn al-Muslim azkar content

Hudhud's morning and evening azkar are based on *Hisn al-Muslim
(Fortress of the Muslim): Invocations from the Qur'an and Sunnah*, compiled by
Sa‘id bin Ali bin Wahf al-Qahtani. The established English edition was
translated by Ismael Ibraheem.

The bundled English translation and transliteration fields were prepared using
version 1.0.3 of
[`@kazishariar/hisnul-muslim-data`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@kazishariar/hisnul-muslim-data)
as a working dataset. That dataset is produced by the Dua Speed Reader Research
Collective and is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Hudhud selects, corrects, normalizes, and adapts the relevant records for its
morning and evening collections; the resulting wording is not represented as
an endorsement by the dataset's authors.

Text and entry alignment were checked against:

- [IslamHouse's English edition of *Hisn al-Muslim*](https://islamhouse.com/ar/books/39062)
- [Sunnah.com's numbered *Hisn al-Muslim* collection](https://sunnah.com/hisn)

Digital sources were accessed on July 23, 2026.
